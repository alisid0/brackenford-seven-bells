# Half-hour test

A test for the developer, written 22 November 2026 for the build at commit `522e651` on `release-prep`. It takes 35 to 45 minutes on a computer. Do not fix anything as you go; write down what happens.

The build is on the **test site** (deployed 22 November 2026; not indexed by search engines). The public site is unchanged.

## How to open it

- The release, 17 stages: https://archery-range-test.vercel.app/?fps=1
- All 29 stages with the special arrows: https://archery-range-test.vercel.app/?release=0&arrows=1
- The release with the new hare: https://archery-range-test.vercel.app/new-hare.html?fps=1

The same pages also run from this folder (double-click `Brackenford-Seven-Bells.html`).

Use Chrome or Edge. On the title card press **Start over** once before you begin, so the counters start from nothing.

## Part 1: the journey as a new player (about 12 minutes)

Open the release. Press **Begin** and play the stages in order with the NEXT sign or the N key, as far as you get in 12 minutes. Do not replay anything yet unless you fail a stage.

Watch for the new line on the result of each stage. It should say how far you were from the next star, for example "38 points to ★★★", and "new best +24" when you beat your own score.

Write down:

1. Did you notice the "points to" line without looking for it? Where did your eye go first on the result?
2. Did the number make you want to shoot AGAIN, or did you ignore it?
3. Which stage was the first that felt too hard, and which felt like filler?
4. How far did you get in 12 minutes?

## Part 2: chasing stars (about 10 minutes)

Now go back with the BACK sign or the B key to stages where you have one or two stars. Pick three of them and try for three stars. Stop a stage when you would honestly stop if nobody were watching.

Write down for each of the three:

5. Stage name, tries, best stars.
6. Why you stopped: got it, bored, arm tired, felt impossible, felt unfair.
7. Could you tell what to do differently on the next try? That is the whole point of the line.

## Part 3: the special arrows (about 6 minutes)

Open the second link (all 29 stages, special arrows). Keys 1 to 4 choose the arrow: plain, Rime (freezes a moving target for a moment), Gale (halves the wind), Kiln (burns away one clump of grass). You get two special shots a stage.

- On **Crossing pair**, freeze a sliding target with Rime and then hit it with a plain arrow.
- On **Crosswind**, shoot once plain and once with Gale. Is the difference plain to see?
- On **Horns in the grass**, burn a clump with Kiln. Does the hare inside become easier?

Write down:

8. For each arrow: did you understand what it did from what you saw, without these notes?
9. Did two charges feel like too few, enough or too many?
10. Did you forget they existed? When?

## Part 4: the new hare (about 4 minutes)

Open the third link and play **First shade** and **The warren** (stages 4 and 7 of the release). The hare here is the new model with the timed bound.

11. Does it read as a hare at 14 m and at 30 m?
12. Does its run look right, or does anything slide, snap or float?
13. Is it fair to hit, or do arrows pass through it or strike empty air?

## Part 5: the pictures (about 6 minutes)

In `assets/concepts/` open `world-map-v1.jpg` and the five aerial views: `aerial-2a-reedwater-pier.jpg`, `aerial-2b-flint-quarry-v2.jpg`, `aerial-3a-alderwood.jpg`, `aerial-3b-bellward-village-v2.jpg`, `aerial-4-stormcrest.jpg`.

For each of the five, one line:

14. Would you want to shoot there: yes, maybe, no. What is the one thing to change?
15. Which place should be built first after the introduction?
16. Which name for the first place: Willowbank Range, Meadowgate Range or Firstlight Range?

## When you finish

Go back to the title card (Esc, or reload) and press **Copy playtest summary**. Paste what it copies into the chat with Claude. It holds counts only: tries, clears, retries, near misses, minutes. It is kept on your computer and sent nowhere.

Then send your answers to the questions, however rough.

## Part 6: in the headset (about 30 minutes more)

Open the first link in the Quest Browser and press **Enter VR**. The test site's progress is separate on each device, so the headset starts from nothing.

Play the stages in order for 15 minutes, then chase three stars on two stages for 10 minutes. Stop when your arm tells you to and note the time.

17. Can you read the "points to" line on the scoreboard to your right and on the plaque on the bow?
18. How many minutes before your bow arm or your shoulders wanted a rest?
19. Read the frames-per-second figure on the scoreboard on **The warren** and **Blighted meadow**. What was the lowest?
20. Open the third link, enter VR, and look at the hare on **First shade**. Does it look right at full size, and is it fair to hit?
21. Special arrows in VR (second link): push the left stick up or down to change arrow. Did you find it without being told? Could you read which arrow was chosen?

Leave VR, go back to the title card and press **Copy playtest summary** in the Quest Browser if you can; if copying is awkward in the headset, just tell Claude roughly how many tries you made.

## Known before you start

- Star thresholds are the old ones (30, 60 and 95 percent). The harder three-star bar in the plan is not in this build.
- The "points to" line and the counters are new today and have only been run by an automated test, not played by a person.
- The special arrows, the new hare and everything in the pictures have never been seen in a headset.
- The six places exist only as pictures. The game still has one range.
