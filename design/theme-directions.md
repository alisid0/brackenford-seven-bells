# Four theme directions (ChatGPT, 22 November 2026)

The developer's instruction on art direction: ChatGPT makes several images, Gemini selects one and writes a theme, and the three models then discuss how to apply it across the levels. These are ChatGPT's four directions, each a key-art picture of the same subject (the archer at a high place looking over the valley toward Stormcrest) so they can be compared. Written down by Claude; ChatGPT cannot write to the repository.
