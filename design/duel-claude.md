# Lantern Rise: Claude's entry

Paper lanterns rise from the valley below the range into the sky. Kindle each one with an arrow before it drifts out of reach. One that gets away is simply gone: nothing is lost.

**Why it is different from Bell Watch:** Bell Watch is defence on the ground, stopping shades before they reach the bell. Lantern Rise is gathering in the air. Every target climbs, so the same lantern is an easy shot low and a proud one high. You choose when to take it.

**Why it is worth a headset:** it plays on height. Lanterns start below eye level in the valley and climb up to 17 m, so you look down the slope, then up into the sky, and lead a target that is rising.

## Rules

| 180 s | nothing new | | | |

- **A lantern** is worth 40, plus 1 for each metre away, plus 4 for each metre above head height.
- **A string** is 3 to 5 lanterns of one colour tied by a thread. Each one you kindle plays the next note up a scale. Kindle the whole string for 120 per lantern. If one gets away, the string is broken, with no penalty.
- **A gold lantern** comes every 22 to 28 s from 35 s and is worth +100. It brings the **Hush**: every lantern hangs still for 5 s.
- **Glow:** hits in a run raise a multiplier, ×1.25 at 6, ×1.5 at 12 and ×2 at 24. A miss never takes it away.
- The bell only rings softly; it is not part of this mode.
- **End cloth:** score and best, then lanterns, strings won and golds, then the highest lantern kindled.

## Scorecard (tests/arcade.html?mode=claude)

All six checks pass: no errors, carefree, nothing behind or nearer than 8 m, at most 9 in view, and skill shows. Scores: idle 0, fair about 7–8k, good about 15–16k (the good bot scores about 2× the fair one). Seconds with nothing to shoot for the fair bot: 0 to 3.5. Frame cost: about 0.5–0.6 ms per frame on a desktop. Not yet tried in a headset.

## For the slot, after the contest

Nothing is needed. One nice-to-have: a mode should be able to tint the sky, for an evening look in its own runs.
