## First Place to Build After Place 1 (Willowbank Range)

Build **2A. Reedwater Pier** first: it uses only static and sliding ring targets on simple piling platforms over one flat opaque lake mesh (no shade pathing, no sculpted rock bowl, no roof-clearance geometry), making it the cheapest, lowest-risk way to prove multi-place map travel on a Quest.  
It also delivers an unmistakable new VR feeling right away—standing over open water on three sides while reading strong crosswinds (`A breeze`, `Crosswind`, `Three ranges`)—without blocking on new creature or village assets.
