## One line
- *If the checks pass:* A free fantasy archery game for desktop browsers and Meta Quest VR: draw, aim and loose through seventeen trials of distance, wind, moving targets and shades that hide and bolt.
- *If experimental:* A free fantasy archery game for desktop browsers, with experimental VR in the Quest Browser: draw, aim and loose through seventeen trials of distance, wind, moving targets and shades that hide and bolt.

## Short description (itch.io or a share preview, about 60 words)
Stand at the line and draw your bow. Bow & Shadow is a short fantasy archery game played in your browser, or in VR through the Quest Browser *(if experimental: "with an experimental VR mode in the Quest Browser")*. Seventeen trials add distance, wind, sliding and pop-up targets, and shadow creatures that hide in the grass and take to the air. Build streaks, earn stars, and watch your best shots again in slow motion. Free, no account, early build.

## Features (only what is in the build)
- Seventeen short archery trials, each about a minute
- Desktop: mouse and keyboard
- VR: Meta Quest Browser with controllers; two-handed draw, or a one-handed mode *(unconfirmed: name only the headsets and input methods that passed on a real headset; if experimental, write "Experimental VR: Meta Quest Browser with controllers" and leave out any draw mode that failed)*
- Left-handed and right-handed play *(on desktop this is in the build; in VR it is unconfirmed until the left-handed check passes)*
- Wind you can read from a pennant
- Streaks, stars and best scores, saved in your browser
- Slow-motion replays of your best shots
- A guided first trial

## Say plainly

## Controls block for the page
Desktop: hold the left mouse button to draw, release to loose. Let go early, or roll the mouse wheel, for a softer shot. N, R and B after a trial: next, again, back.
VR *(unconfirmed until the Quest checks pass; leave out any control that failed)*: the bow is in your hand. Bring your free hand to the string, hold the trigger, pull back and let go. Click the right stick for one-handed draw. Shoot the signs after a trial.
