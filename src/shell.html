Brackenford · archery

The Seven Bells

The range: stand at the line and shoot your way up twenty stages of distance, wind and moving marks. Hits in a row build a streak, and letting go just as the draw completes is a perfect release.

Roam the vale: walk where you like, train in the yard, climb, and wake the seven bells.

 In VR. Two-handed is real archery: free hand to the string, hold its trigger, pull back, let go. Single-handed draws on a held trigger alone. Clicking the right stick swaps them in the headset.

 A right-handed archer holds the bow in the left hand. In VR, simply take the bow with whichever hand you prefer.
