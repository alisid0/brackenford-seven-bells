# Mixed reality: the look (ChatGPT, 22 November 2026)

Companion to Gemini's `design/mixed-reality.md`. Written down by Claude. **A design note, not a promise: nothing here has been checked in the Quest's browser or on a headset, and no mixed-reality code exists.**

Picture: `assets/concepts/mr-bell-in-the-room.jpg` (concept B from the player's eyes in a lamp-lit living room).

- **Strongest feel, and what to try first:** B, Bell in the Room. Both models agree. "A familiar room becomes the site of one complete Silent Peal encounter, with a clear payoff when the iron bell turns bronze." The Belfry Window has the strongest view and can follow once placement and contrast have been tried on a Quest.
- **Keeping flat shapes readable over a real room:** start with the 0.75 m bell at 2.6 m and a crow about 0.45 m across the wings. Lighter plum facets on the crow's wings; a narrow lighter bevel on the iron bell. Keep mid-tones bright enough to survive a dim camera view, with cream the brightest accent. A soft, tight contact shadow under each foot of the frame. **Avoid** thick outlines, bloom, broad synthetic shadows and tinting the whole room.
- **The cracks in the wall:** treat them as thorn marks, not damage. Two short tapered plum shapes floating just off the wall, each with a lighter plum edge and a few angular barbs. The crow comes out in front of the mark. The wall stays whole and visible round it: no displacement, rubble or hole.
- **The briefing card** (cream cloth, plum lettering):
  1. Stay in place and face the bell.
  2. Hit three shade crows.
  3. Ring the freed bell.
  4. Press Hide to clear the room.

ChatGPT's caveats on its own picture: the bow came out curved (the game's is straight) and the floor shadow is broader than intended.

Claude's note: Gemini's briefing has four safety lines (check a metre round your arms and overhead; stay in one spot; watch for people and pets; a button hides the game). ChatGPT's card is the task, not the safety. A real version needs both; safety first.
