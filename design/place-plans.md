# Placement Plans for Places 2–6 (Aerial Views)

Metric plans for the five places beyond Willowbank Range (`design/world-plan.md`), incorporating the three accepted stage moves (`The gauntlet → Stormcrest`; `Sliding mark ↔ Three ranges` swapped between Pier and Quarry) and the stand comfort rules (`design/vr-stand-rules.md`).

**Unified coordinates:** `(along, right, y)` in metres from archer's stance centre `(0, 0)`: `along +` forward (`-` behind); `right +` right (`-` left); standing eye = `y_stance + 1.6 m` (seated `+1.15 m`). Every scoreboard and wind pennant sits **inside `±42°`** of centre while staying clear of all target rays.
