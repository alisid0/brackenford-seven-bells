> why is everyone acting like this is the only stage? The stage that was there yesterday, give it a name that should just be in the introductory level. ## Travel

Brambleford opens the Pier and the Quarry. Clearing both opens Alderwood and Bellward Village, in either order. Clearing both of those opens Stormcrest. Travel returns the player to the map and fades straight to the chosen stance; the player never moves through the world.

The dotted paths in the picture include a direct line from Brambleford to the middle junction; the travel order above governs, not the drawn lines.

## Comfort rules every stance obeys

From `design/vr-stand-rules.md` (Gemini): the stance never moves; solid footing at least 2.4 × 2.2 m; front edge about 1 m ahead; a low rail; no glass floors; water under boards flows slowly and sideways. The Pier and Stormcrest show what is below past the rail, with solid boards or stone underfoot.

## What this changes

- The aerial view and the seven player's-eye pictures already made (`aerial-v1.jpg`, `pov-v1` … `pov-v7`) describe **Brambleford Range only**, and its stand becomes a deck over a channel.
- Each other place needs its own plan in metres, aerial view and player's-eye pictures, in the agreed order of work (`Team.md`), before anything is built.
- The stage order within the release changes (stages are grouped by place). The order stays a configurable list.
- The game code has one range today. Several places is new work for after the pictures are approved.
