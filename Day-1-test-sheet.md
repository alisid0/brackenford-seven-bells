# Day 1 test sheet

Test the release build on the test site: https://archery-range-test.vercel.app/?fps=1 (commit `69c6fde`). It plays the 17 release stages, so go by stage name. The frame counter shows on screen, on the scoreboard and on the bow plaque.
Allow about 45 minutes on the computer and 60–90 minutes on the Quest. Don't fix anything; just write down what happens.

## Before you start
- Which headset: Quest 2, 3 or 3S?
- On the title card choose "Start over" once, so both tests begin from nothing.

## Part 1 — computer (Chrome or Edge)
1. Open the link in a private window. Does the title card make sense without explanation?
2. Press "Begin". Play the first stage, following the instructions on screen. Did you know what to do? What confused you?
3. Play the stages in order using the NEXT sign or the N key, as far as you can get. Each one opens when you earn a star on the one before.
4. If you get stuck on a stage, note which one, then open https://archery-range-test.vercel.app/?release=0 (all 29 stages, any order) to try First shade, Horns in the grass, On the post and Flush.
5. Reload the page. Are your stars and best scores still there?
6. Try: letting go early for a soft shot, the mouse wheel while drawing, the H key, the Esc key.

## Part 2 — Quest (Quest Browser)
New in this build: instructions appear on a low board in front of you during the first stage. Is it readable, and is it in the way of your shot?
1. Open the link, press Enter VR. Is the bow in your hand? Which hand?
2. Stage 1, two-handed: free hand to the string, hold trigger, pull back, let go.
3. Can you read the scoreboard to the right, and the plaque on the bow?
4. Finish stage 1. Did a replay play? Were you returned to the line? Did the BACK, AGAIN and NEXT signs appear, and did shooting one work?
5. Flick the left stick right. Did it go to the next stage?
6. Click the right stick. Did it switch to single-handed draw? Hold a trigger and let go.
7. Squeeze the grip on your other hand at the bow. Did the bow change hands?
8. Play the stages in order as far as you can get.
9. On The warren (four shades) and Blighted meadow (the last stage): does it stutter when you turn your head? Read the frames-per-second figure on the scoreboard.
10. Take the headset off for ten seconds and put it back on. Does play resume?
11. Leave VR and press Enter VR again. Does it work a second time?

## What to report back
For each stage you played, one line:

`stage number | platform | finished? | stars | too easy / about right / too hard / unfair | anything broken`

And these, each in a sentence:
1. Headset model.
2. Anything that did not work at all, with the stage and what you did just before.
3. The first moment you were confused, on the computer and in VR.
4. Frame rate: smooth, occasional stutter, or constant stutter, and on which stages. If the Quest's developer overlay is switched on, the frames-per-second figure on The warren and Blighted meadow.
5. Comfort: any dizziness, and when (replay, stage change, anything else).
6. Arms: could you play ten stages in a row without tiring?
7. Readability: scoreboard, signs, plaque, pop-up scores.
8. Sound: anything too loud, too quiet or annoying.
9. Your three favourite stages and your three least favourite.
10. Whether the shades were easy enough to see without their smoke.
